Privacy Policy for FlowMint

Last updated: October 11, 2026

FlowMint ("we," "our," or "the App") is a mobile application for tracking spending, income, savings, and financial activities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use the App:

  • Financial data you enter manually, such as income, expenses, wallet balances, categories, and notes
  • Profile details such as your name, preferred currency, and monthly budget
  • Device information, such as app version, operating system, and crash/error data
  • SMS or notification data only if you explicitly enable the app's transaction import features

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Show your transaction history and financial summaries
  • Provide budgeting, savings, and analytics features
  • Improve app stability, performance, and user experience
  • Support SMS/notification-based expense import when enabled

3. Local Storage and Processing

Most data used by FlowMint is stored locally on your device. We do not require a user account or cloud login to use the app. The app may store your data in the device's local storage for the app to function correctly.

4. SMS and Notification Access

If you enable SMS or notification import, FlowMint may access SMS/notification content to detect transactions and improve tracking. This access is used only to parse transaction details and is not used to read or share unrelated personal messages beyond what is needed for the transaction import feature.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We do not share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing or resale purposes.

We may share limited information only when required by law, court order, or to protect the safety and security of our users.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, or app distribution. These services may collect technical information such as device type, OS version, and app usage statistics. We do not use these services to sell or expose your financial records.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information stored in the App. However, no mobile app or digital system can be completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your device secure and protecting your app access.

8. Children's Privacy

FlowMint is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

9. Your Choices

You can:

  • Delete your app data from within the app settings
  • Disable SMS or notification imports
  • Stop using the app at any time
